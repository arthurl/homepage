About me

I am currently a software engineer at Singpass in the Government Technology Agency of Singapore (GovTech), building a data platform for a national digital identity service.

Previously, I was a quantitative developer at Eisler Capital and Rokos Capital Management (RCM), as well as a functional programmer at Standard Chartered Bank (SCB).

Before that, I was a data scientist / research engineer at the Data Analytics Department of Institute for Infocomm Research (I2R), a research assistant at Data Storage Institute (DSI), a master’s student at the Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics (in University of Waterloo), and a scholar at Oxford University.
